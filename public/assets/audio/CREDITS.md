# Second Value audio credits

These files are bundled with the game and served from the same Site. Audio is optional; sound effects and background music have independent controls and start off for first-time visitors.

## Paper cue — auction-paper-v1.mp3

- Original: "Pages qu'on tourne #7" by Joseph SARDIN.
- Source: https://lasonotheque.org/pages-qu-on-tourne-7-s2214.html
- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Recording: real paper handling, as documented on the source page.
- Changes: excerpt from 0.10 seconds, 0.92 seconds long; mono, gain adjustment, peak limiting, short edge fades, MP3 encoding.

## Gavel cue — auction-gavel-v1.mp3

- Original: "Marteau de president, 1 coup" by Joseph SARDIN.
- Source: https://lasonotheque.org/marteau-de-president-1-coup-s1588.html
- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Recording: a real wooden gavel and its block, as documented on the source page.
- Changes: first 0.29 seconds; mono, gain adjustment, peak limiting, short edge fades, MP3 encoding.

- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## Background music — old-harbor-v1.mp3

- Original: "Soft Mysterious Harp Loop" by Jordy Hake (VWolfdog).
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/soft-mysterious-harp-loop
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Changes: full 54.91-second track, gain adjustment, peak limiting, brief edge fades for repeated playback, and MP3 transcoding. Runtime plays the track quietly at 18% volume.
- The game also displays the title, author, source and license link, with an editing notice, in both its solo and multiplayer "How to play" dialogs.

No endorsement by the original creators is implied. The CC BY 3.0 track and our edited version remain available under that license; the game imposes no additional restriction on these audio files.
