THE RULES

One table.
Different reasons to bid.

  1. Invite two to four players. Each starts with 1,000 virtual coins. Pick one role before the host begins.
  2. Everyone bids on the same crate of six objects. The whole crate goes to one winner.
  3. Choose from six roles. Everyone shares the same bidding actions; each role has one exclusive ability.
  4. The Appraiser may privately identify two objects per crate. The Restorer repairs after winning; the Collector delivers a pair in a locked theme.
  5. The Salvager harvests reproductions; the Curator exhibits three different categories; the Antique Dealer sells to a regular customer.
  6. Round 1 shows silhouettes. Round 2 adds the genuine count. Round 3 reveals one identity and the repairable count.
  7. Quotes stay secret until everyone submits. Withdraw and edit while others are still deciding. The last submission reveals all bids immediately.
  8. Early sale requires 1.8× the runner-up in round 1, or 1.3× in round 2. Highest bid wins round 3. Ties use the published seat order.
  9. Only the winner pays. They choose how to sell; everyone else waits for settlement. Cash and profit carry to the next crate.
  10. Refresh to reconnect in the same tab. A disconnected player must return before the room can continue.

All coins are virtual. No chat or voice required.
