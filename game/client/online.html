How to return to this room
  1. Return in the original browser tab, then refresh it or retry the connection.
  2. A room code alone cannot restore your previous seat in a new tab.
  3. Rooms expire after six hours with no player activity.
  4. Offline status begins after 15 seconds without a room update.
  5. Bids, sales and host actions never happen automatically while someone is offline.
  1. Invite two to four players. Each starts with 1,000 virtual coins. Pick one role before the host begins.
  2. Everyone bids on the same crate of six objects. The whole crate goes to one winner.
  3. New sessions: The auction house publishes a reserve before bidding, around 10% of the crate’s ordinary sale value (8–12%). Role abilities do not affect it. It stays fixed for all three rounds; even a sole bid must meet it. If the crate goes unsold, nobody pays.
  4. Choose from six roles. Everyone shares the same bidding actions; each role has one exclusive ability.
  5. New sessions: Each crate has a public source. The source changes draw probabilities, not guaranteed contents. Identities and ordinary prices are fixed before bidding.
  6. After settlement, compare the buyer’s actual result with other players’ hypothetical returns at the same winning bid. Fees and available cash are included; these comparisons do not change anyone’s balance.
  7. The Appraiser may privately identify two objects per crate. The Restorer repairs after winning; the Collector delivers a pair in a locked theme.
  8. The Salvager harvests reproductions; the Curator exhibits three different categories; the Antique Dealer sells to a regular customer.
  9. Round 1 shows silhouettes. Round 2 adds the genuine count. Round 3 reveals one identity and the repairable count.
  10. Quotes stay secret until everyone submits. Withdraw and edit while others are still deciding. The last submission reveals all bids immediately.
  11. Early sale requires 1.8× the runner-up in round 1, or 1.3× in round 2. Highest bid wins round 3. Ties use the published seat order.
  12. Only the winner pays. They choose how to sell; everyone else waits for settlement. Cash and profit carry to the next crate.
  13. Refresh to reconnect in the same tab. A disconnected player must return before the room can continue.

All coins are virtual. No chat or voice required.
